Scoring guide & quick start

Enter athlete names, choose a mode and pitch location, then complete the outcome and direction or mechanics. Tap Record Attempt. Location stays selected; other choices clear after every attempt. Each athlete and each mode maintains independent counters.

Hitting: Contact = (Barrel + Contact) / scored swings. Barrel = Barrel / scored swings. Fouls are excluded from all scoring and scored swing counts. Correct direction = correct fair balls / all fair balls in play. Inside → Pull, Middle → Middle, Outside → Opposite. Fouls remain in the attempt log only; fouls and misses are excluded from direction. A miss does not require direction.

Catching: Blocking = (Controlled + Blocked) / all attempts. Controlled = Controlled / all attempts. Proper positioning = Correct / all attempts, including missed blocks. Controlled means the ball stays within playable distance; Blocked means stopped but not controlled. Use glove and throwing-hand side for either handedness.

Undo removes the last recorded attempt in the selected mode. Reset Session clears that athlete’s current attempts in both modes after confirmation; saved tests remain. Clear Session erases all athletes’ current attempts and saved tests after confirmation. Unfinished choices are never included in exports. “—” means there are no eligible attempts.
